What We Do

Emergency Ambulance Service

When every second counts, Annai Foundation provides prompt and reliable ambulance service. Our fleet of well-equipped ambulances, staffed with trained paramedics and drivers, ensures timely medical care and safe transportation to hospitals. Available 24/7, our ambulance service is a lifeline for those in need of urgent assistance.

Social Welfare

We provide essential resources like food assistance, housing support, and financial aid to individuals and families in need, helping them improve their quality of life.

Health Care

Our foundation offers access to basic and supplemental medical services, including community health centers and mobile health vans that bring care directly to underserved areas.

Child Welfare

We offer child protection programs, support for children at risk of social exclusion, and safe spaces for vulnerable youth to grow and thrive.

Education

We provide educational support such as tutoring, school supplies, scholarships, and access to digital learning tools to help students succeed academically.

Community Development

We engage in initiatives focused on community organization, infrastructure development, and supporting local groups to strengthen communities from within.

Livelihood Support

We offer job training, skills development programs, and micro-financing opportunities to help individuals achieve economic independence and self-sufficiency.

Advocacy

Annai Foundation actively advocates for policy changes and social reforms that benefit marginalized and vulnerable populations through awareness campaigns and partnerships.

Disaster Response

We play a critical role in providing immediate relief and long-term recovery support following natural disasters or emergencies, ensuring affected communities receive essential aid and resources.
