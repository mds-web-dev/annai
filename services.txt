Our Services

1)Emergency Ambulace Service:
When every second count, the annai foundation is here to provide and reliable ambulance service.

Our fleet of well-equipped ambulance, staffed with trained paramedics and drives, ensure timely medical care and safe transporation to hospitals.

Available 24/7, our ambulance service is a lifetime or lifeline for those who in need of urgent assistance.

2)Social Welfare:
This includes providing essential resources like food assistance, housing support and financial aid to those in need.

3)Health Care:
Our foundation offer access to basic and supplement medical service, including medical centres and mobile health vans.

4)Child Welfare:
Service may includes child protection, support for children at risk of social exlusion, and programs for children in need.

5)Education:
Our Foundation may provide educational support, including tutoring, school supplies, and access to educational resources.

6)Community Development:
This includes initiatives like community organisation infrastructure development, and support for local organisation.

7)Livelihood Support:
Foundation may offer job training-skills development and financial support to help individuals become self-sufficient.

8)Advocacy:
Our foundation actively advocate for policy changes that benefit vulnerable populations.

9)Disaster Response:
Our foundation often play a vital role in providing immediate relief and long-term support following natural disasters (or) emergencies.
