About Annai Foundation

We are driven by a deep commitment to make a difference in the lives of individuals and communities. Inspired by compassion and service, our foundation was established to address the pressing needs of society, offering hope, support, and care to those who need it most. Annai Foundation provides a wide range of public services aimed at supporting and assisting vulnerable individuals, families, and communities. Our initiatives often focus on key social issues such as poverty, education, and housing — all with the goal of improving overall well-being. Our mission is to create a lasting impact by offering essential services, fostering empowerment, and promoting holistic wellness. With a dedicated team of professionals and volunteers, we consistently strive to uphold the values of empathy, inclusivity, and sustainability in everything we do.

Our Achievements

Reuniting Families

Our foundation has assisted individuals living on the street who were separated from their families by contacting their relatives and helping to reunite them.

Comprehensive Support

We have conducted final rites for 320 individuals, provided emergency assistance to save approximately 250 accident victims, and supported destitute elderly people by admitting them to care homes and fulfilling their needs.
